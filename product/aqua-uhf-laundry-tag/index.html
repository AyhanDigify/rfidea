Aqua Series UHF Laundry Tag

Aqua SeriesTextile tag with φ5 mm PCB module

A washable textile laundry tag built around a rugged φ5 mm PCB chip module for maximum durability in industrial laundries. Available in multiple lengths and installable by sewing, pouch, heat-pressing or hanging.

  • Rugged PCB chip module
  • Designed for industrial laundry
  • Sew, pouch, heat-press or hang
  • Custom sizes and printing

Technical specifications

Air interface protocolEPC Class 1 Gen 2 / ISO/IEC 18000-63
Frequency860–960 MHz (UHF)
Chip moduleφ5 mm PCB
Available sizes86×16 · 70×17 · 70×30 · 70×15 · 70×10 · 60×9 · 60×20 · 58×30 · 58×15 · 50×12 · 36×11 · 35×35 · 30×10 mm
InstallationSewing, pouch, heat-pressing, hanging
Surface customisationUV laser engraving, UV or thermal transfer printing, inkjet

Typical applications

  • Hospitality — hotel linens, towels, robes, uniforms
  • Healthcare — hospital linen, patient gowns, surgical textiles
  • Industrial — workwear, rental uniforms, cleanroom garments
  • Sports & leisure — towel rental, locker management
  • Laundry service providers — automated sorting, billing, loss prevention
