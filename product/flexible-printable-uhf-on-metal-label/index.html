On-Metal RFID Labels

Flexible & Printable UHF On-Metal Label

OM-FXAvailable from 30 × 15 mm to 103 × 52 mm

A flexible foam-spacer on-metal label series available in a wide range of sizes and chips. Supplied on rolls for print-and-encode with industrial RFID printers, it conforms to curved metal surfaces such as cylinders, pipes and equipment housings.

  • Flexible — conforms to curved metal surfaces
  • Wide size range for every asset
  • Choice of Impinj, NXP, Alien and FM chips
  • Roll format for RFID printers
