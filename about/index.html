About RFIDEA

RFIDEA is a Netherlands-based identification specialist. We bring together RFID technology, durable industrial labels and thermal transfer printing supplies — so our customers can identify, track and trace anything, anywhere.

100+products across RFID, labels & ribbons

Our mission

Make identification work where it is hardest.

Industrial identification fails in predictable places: on metal, around liquids, in the wash, in the furnace and in the freezer. RFIDEA focuses on exactly those places. We combine an engineered product portfolio with hands-on application advice, so the tag, label or ribbon you choose performs from day one.

Our portfolio spans passive UHF and NFC RFID — on-metal labels, PCB and hard tags, laundry tags and battery-free sensor tags — alongside high-temperature, strong-adhesive and cryogenic labels, and a full range of wax, wax/resin, resin and textile thermal transfer ribbons.

  • Application-driven product selection and testing
  • Customisation: sizes, adhesives, printing, UV laser engraving, encoding
  • Products manufactured under ISO 9001 / ISO 14001 quality systems
  • REACH and RoHS compliant materials
