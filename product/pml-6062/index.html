High Temperature Polyimide Label (70 Micron Silicon Adhesive)

Construction & performance

Face stockThis product is a PI anti-static label with a permanent silicone pressure-sensitive adhesive. The product is electrostatic safe, with a surface resistance and adhesive resistance of 10⁶-10¹⁰ ohms and a moving peel voltage of less than 100 volts.
