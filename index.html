Why RFIDEA

Application expertise first. Product second.

A tag is only as good as its fit with the surface, temperature, chemistry and reader environment. We start with your process and recommend — then sample — the product that will perform.

About RFIDEA

Application engineering

Tag and antenna selection based on surface, liquid content, temperature and read-range requirements.

Customisation

Custom sizes, die-cuts, adhesives, pre-printing, UV laser engraving and EPC encoding.

Samples & fast supply

Test samples for validation and fast supply of standard products.

Compliance

EPC Gen2 RFID, REACH / RoHS materials, food-contact ribbons and MRI-tested laundry tags.
